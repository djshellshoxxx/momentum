# Momentum, explained simply

Momentum is a compressor for music. A regular compressor mostly asks, “How loud is the sound right now?” Momentum also asks, “How quickly did the loudness just change?”

That lets it react strongly to a sudden jump, like a snare hit or a shout, while responding less to a slow, smooth rise in volume. Think of it like a bouncer who pays special attention to sudden rushes rather than everyone who gradually walks in.

The controls let you adjust how sensitive and strong that reaction is, how quickly it happens, and how it recovers. The project’s own guide describes VST3 and standalone versions.
